Cell model: O'Hara–Rudy CiPA v1.0 (Dutta et al. 2017), endocardial, CL 2000 ms, 400 pre-paced beats. Drug binding: Li et al. 2017 dynamic hERG Markov model with the FDA's published per-compound parameters; six further channels blocked by a static Hill equation from Li2017 IC50s. qNet = ∫(INaL+ICaL+Ito+IKr+IKs+IK1)dt. Scope: 12 compounds, not 28 — the fitted dynamic hERG binding parameters exist only for the CiPA training set. Preclinical compound screening; not a medical device and not a claim about any individual.
